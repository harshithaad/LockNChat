          Encryption

          Messages are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from an ECDH P-256 exchange. The server only relays and stores ciphertext and cannot read your messages.

          Safety number

          Compare this number with your contact in person or on a call. If it matches on both devices, nobody is intercepting your conversation.
